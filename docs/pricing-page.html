Pricing FAQ

The short version: the free plan is the real product, and you only pay to scale it.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Free forever, no credit card, no time limit. The local engine is the actual product, not a crippled demo. Solo developers can do real work on it indefinitely.

Does my code leave my machine?

On Free, never. On Pro and Team, only what you choose to sync: your code map and the files you ask about, encrypted in transit and at rest. Source is never used to train models. Enterprise can run the whole thing on-prem or in your own cloud.

How do you measure the token savings you claim?

On every session, FACTS counts the tokens in the context it assembles versus what your agent would have spent searching the codebase itself. You see the number in your dashboard. You don't take our word for it.

What counts as a seat on Team?

One active developer per month. Managers and stakeholders who only view dashboards are free, so leadership can watch the analytics without paying a developer seat.

Can I try Pro or Team first?

Yes. Both have a 14-day free trial with no credit card. Cancel anytime, and you drop straight back to the Free plan with everything local intact.

Which languages are supported?

Core languages (TypeScript and JavaScript, Python, and more) on Free. The full language set on Pro and Team. New languages ship regularly.

Do you offer discounts?

Pro is free for verified students and open-source maintainers. Annual billing saves 20% on Pro and Team. Volume pricing is available on Enterprise.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep the Free plan forever, and the local tool keeps working offline. Your cloud data stays exportable for 30 days after you downgrade.
