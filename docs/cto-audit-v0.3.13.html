The short version

Two claims on the savings panel were false. Both are fixed.

1. "Codebase tokens are exact (cl100k, from the analyzer)." They are not. The analyzer is Math.round(text.length / 3.5) — a character estimate its own source documents as tracking cl100k "within ~8%". Nothing tokenizes anything. The caption now says both sides are estimates.

2. "each verified against the vendor's own page." True of 22 of 24 rows. Two ship as aggregator precisely because the vendor page could not be confirmed, yet the rate card printed the word "verified" for them too. The caption now counts the primary rows, and unconfirmed rows read "checked" with a † marker.

A third finding made the product's headline number smaller.

The two halves of the savings ratio used different divisors — the codebase by 3.5 (the analyzer), the artifact by 4 (the panel). That made the artifact look ~14% smaller than the same rule would make the codebase, inflating both the multiplier and the percentage. The old code defended this as "the conservative direction (it makes FACTS look worse, not better)", which was backwards: a smaller artifact is a bigger saving.

Both sides now use the analyzer's divisor. On this repo the headline fell from 5.1× / 80% to 4.5× / 78% — smaller, and actually true.

Six of the eight blocker claims did not survive.

Refuted claims included "the page can hide a real CVE" (a non-npm CVE cannot reach that field in the first place), "the weekly workflow grants a standing production deploy" (its publish step is gated on secrets that do not exist in this repo), and "the chart hides that models tokenize differently" (the method is disclosed directly under the chart). Each was checked against the code rather than accepted.
