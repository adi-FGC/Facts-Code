These are blind-panel scores from the A/B/C experiment: an untouched control, a single-pass Claude rewrite, a metric-gated auto-tinker loop, and the hybrid merge. Each time the loop re-runs locally and produces a better best, this page is rebuilt and the flagship + scores update. That's the "improves with time" — verified offline, then published here.

How it works. The evaluator scores the manual against a fixed 10-dimension rubric grouped into three rollups (reusable · enforceable · honest), computed deterministically from the model's per-dimension scores. It never persists your key or your document. "Improve" runs one generate→re-score pass so you can see the delta. Honest limits: an LLM judge's verdict can shift with framing; treat scores as a strong signal, not a proof, and re-run to gauge variance.
